What does 4.5:1 mean?

Contrast ratio compares how light the text is with how light its background is. It goes from 1:1 (same colour, invisible) to 21:1 (black on white). The web accessibility guideline WCAG sets these minimums:

  • 4.5:1 for normal text (level AA)
  • 3:1 for large text, 24px or bigger, or bold 18.66px (level AA)
  • 7:1 for the best readability (level AAA)

Colour vision deficiency changes which colours look different, but light against dark still works for almost everyone. That is why contrast, not colour, is what keeps text readable.
