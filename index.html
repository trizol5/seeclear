Welcome to SeeClear

About SeeClear

SeeClear is a website dedicated to the health and welfare of the visually impaired community. We take a clear stand in support of visually impaired people and their right to equal access to information. The site helps students understand the everyday difficulties visually impaired readers face when reading text materials. It also shows how color choices and contrast can make public text materials easy or hard to read. In doing so, SeeClear reminds presenters and creators of public materials to avoid color combinations that harm readability for visually impaired audiences.

Why it matters?

Good colour contrast makes text easier to read for everyone, including people with visual impairments. Learn how colour choices can affect the accessibility of websites, posters, and presentations.
