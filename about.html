    Tips

    • Never use colour alone to show meaning: add a word, an icon or a pattern.
    • Body text needs a contrast of at least 4.5:1; large headings need 3:1.
    • Red and green, and light colours on yellow or white, are the pairs that fail most often.
    • Check your poster or slides in the Checker before printing.
