Color Accessibility Evaluator

This module aims to provide a checking and evaluation service for images and PDFs created by students and staff at CityUHK, to ensure the inclusiveness of text and background colours.

5 steps to use:
  1. Upload.
  2. Select color.
  3. If you don't want to upload your document, you can also directly choose the color below the upload region.
  4. Click "Start Evaluation" button.
  5. Get the result and suggestions.
